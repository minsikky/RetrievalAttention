- Tried two alternatives to the current online insertion signal on generated-memory decode:

    - `45411705` current `online_next`

- Outcome:
  - replacement-style future-window weakened retrieval too much
  - additive hybrid preserved `e96` quality only when it made the graph denser and slower, then failed at `e192`

- Current conclusion:
  - future-window evidence is not a useful insertion-signal upgrade in its tested forms
  - the workspace source has been reverted to the stable `online_next` path
  - current online baseline remains:
    - birth-step provenance only
    - no future-window signal logic in source
